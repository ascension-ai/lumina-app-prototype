A DIFFERENT KIND OF AUTOMATION

Not another chatbot.
A system that remembers to look.

Chat waits for a question. Traditional automation breaks the moment work gets ambiguous. Lumina handles the middle: recurring responsibilities that need context, judgment, and restraint.

Tell it what deserves attention once. It turns that intent into an inspectable runbook, follows it on schedule, and keeps the noise out of your day.
